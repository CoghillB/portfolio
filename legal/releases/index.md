## Granted — how to fill that column

List only what the client actually ticked, abbreviated: `name`, `logo`, `quote`,
`screenshots`, `figures`. Write `figures (3)` and itemise them in section F of
`../claims-log.md`.

An item not listed here was not granted. **A blank is a no, not an oversight to be
resolved by assumption.**

## Status values

- `active` — permission current, case study may be published
- `revoked` — withdrawn. Keep the row, add the date, unpublish per the revocation
  steps in `README.md`
